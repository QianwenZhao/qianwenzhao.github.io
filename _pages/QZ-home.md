I am a robotics researcher and engineer passionate about developing human-centered robotic systems. My work focuses on robotics simulation, robotic hand design, physical human-robot interaction, and systems that help robots collaborate more naturally and safely with people.

I hold a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Stevens Institute of Technology, where I developed a strong foundation in robotics, modeling, control, and experimental system design. Through my research and projects, I enjoy turning ideas into working robotic platforms, from simulation environments to physical prototypes.
